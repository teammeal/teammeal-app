Why Big Group Orders Go Wrong

The food is rarely the problem. The ordering and the checks are.

Chaotic Ordering

Choices arrive as texts, screenshots, and replies buried in a thread. Someone always forgets, and an allergy gets lost along the way.

Kitchens Caught Off Guard

A 20-meal order that shows up all at once is hard for any kitchen to plan around, especially at the lunch rush.

Servers Stuck Waiting

Staff stand at the table while everyone reads the menu, decides, and figures out whose check is whose. That slows down every other table too.

Long Waits for Everyone

When the order lands late and unorganized, the whole team sits hungry while the kitchen catches up.

How Team Meal Fixes It

One link replaces the thread, the spreadsheet, and the guesswork.

Share One Link

Paste the restaurant menu, set an order deadline, and send the link to your team, class, office, or family.

Everyone Picks Ahead of Time

Each person reads the menu on their own phone, chooses a meal, and adds allergies or special requests. No one decides at the table, and no account is needed.

Send the Kitchen One Order Ahead of Time

Totals are grouped by item, requests stay attached, and allergy flags stand out. The order also shows who ordered what, so the check is easy to split. The kitchen can plan before you arrive, and your server has nothing to wait on.

Built for the Whole Table

Kitchen-ready, not just organizer-ready. Every person in the chain gets what they need.

For Organizers

See who has ordered and who hasn't, and stop retyping everyone's choices into one message.

For Participants

For Kitchens and Servers

Get a clear list with item counts, notes, and allergy flags, plus advance notice. A large party no longer ties up a server or slows down every other table.

Made for Teams, Offices, and Families
