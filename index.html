Why Big Group Orders Go Wrong

Restaurants and servers aren't ready for a large party. No one knows what they want, and no one knows whose check is whose.

Kitchens Caught Off Guard

A large order that shows up all at once is hard for any kitchen to plan around, and the scramble slows every other order.

Servers Stuck Waiting

Staff stand at the table while everyone reads the menu, decides, and figures out whose check is whose. That slows down every other table too.

Long Waits for Everyone

When the order lands late and unorganized, the whole team sits hungry while the kitchen catches up.

How Team Meal Fixes It

One link replaces the guesswork.

Share One Link

Paste the restaurant menu, set an order deadline, and send the link to your team, class, office, or family.

Everyone Picks Ahead of Time

Each person reads the menu on their own phone, chooses exactly what they want, and adds allergies or special requests. No one decides at the table, and no account is needed.

Send the Kitchen One Order Ahead of Time

Totals are grouped by item, requests stay attached, and allergy flags stand out. The order also shows who ordered what, so the check is easier to split. The kitchen can plan before you arrive, and your server has nothing to wait on.

Built for the Whole Table

Kitchen-ready, not just organizer-ready. Every person gets what they need.

For Organizers

See who has ordered and who hasn’t, plan ahead and make sure everything runs smoothly.

For Participants

Pick what you want, add your allergy note, and know your request will reach the kitchen.

For Kitchens and Servers

Get a clear list with item counts, notes, and allergy flags, plus advance notice. A large party no longer ties up a server or slows down every other table.

Made for Teams, Offices, and Families

Youth sports lunches, office catering, class parties, family reunions, and any meal where more than a few people need to order from the same place.
